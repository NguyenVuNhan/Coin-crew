Group fund wireframes

Sport group Cầu lông Thứ 5. Xanh and Lan are admins; Xanh is the person looking unless a caption says otherwise. The fund is one shared pool the admins keep: 400.000₫ = 650.000₫ added (200k walk-ins + 300k Minh's gift + 150k Lan) − 250.000₫ spent (150k drinks + 100k shuttlecocks). Phones are 375px wide (drawn smaller). 🐷 stands for the Lucide piggy-bank icon added to icons.tsx.
