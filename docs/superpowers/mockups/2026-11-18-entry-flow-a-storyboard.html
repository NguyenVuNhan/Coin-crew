3 · Try it

  • Type an amount and press Xong: the amount folds into one line and the categories appear.
  • Pick a category: it folds too, and the details line and Save appear.
  • Tap any folded line to reopen it; nothing you typed is lost.
  • Switch Chi ⇄ Thu: the amount stays, the category is cleared and the tiles reopen.
  • Vay/Nợ shows today's separate people-and-split form, unchanged in this plan.
  • On PC (mouse), the page stays as it is today, with nothing folded.
